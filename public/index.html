How it runs

Art in Monday. Boxes out Friday.

  1. 01

    Send the art

    A vector file is ideal, but a napkin photo works too. Tell us the garment, the quantity and the deadline.

  2. 02

    We punch the file

    Your logo gets rebuilt stitch by stitch for the fabric it's going on. Knit and canvas need different files — we make both.

  3. 03

    Stitch-out proof

    We sew a real sample on the real garment and send you a photo. Nothing runs in bulk until you say go.

  4. 04

    Run, trim, fold, ship

    Backing trimmed clean, every piece inspected, folded by size and boxed, then shipped anywhere.

Questions

Before you ask

Can I bring my own garments?

Yes, and plenty of people do. We just ask that you send one extra piece per style in case of a machine fault — embroidery goes through the fabric, so a mistake isn't something we can wash out.

What's digitizing, and why does it cost extra?

Your logo has to be rebuilt as a stitch path — direction, density, underlay, pull compensation — before a machine can sew it. It's a person doing it, not a converter button. It's a one-time cost per design, it's free on your first order, and the file is yours to keep.

How small can my text go?

About a quarter inch tall for clean block letters on a stable fabric. Below that, thread thickness wins and letters fill in. On piqué knit or fleece, give it a little more room. We'll flag it in the proof if something's going to close up.

Do you ship outside Arizona?

Regularly — most of the country is two to three days on ground, and valley-wide delivery is free on bulk orders.

What if the color is wrong?

Send Pantone numbers if you have them and we'll match to the closest polyester thread, then show you the stitch-out before the run. If we missed the mark on our end, we re-sew it on us.
